# Billing FAQ

## When are invoices issued?

Invoices are generated on the first business day of each month and cover usage
from the previous calendar month.

## Can we pay by purchase order?

Yes. Enter the PO number in the billing profile before the 25th. Purchase-order
invoices are due within 30 days.

## How do we change the billing contact?

Workspace administrators can update the billing contact from the account settings
page. Changes take effect on the next invoice.

## What happens after a failed payment?

We retry the card three times over ten days. After the third failure the workspace
enters a read-only grace period for fourteen days.
