# Partner Integrations Directory

The integrations directory lists every partner whose connector is generally
available to Northwind Labs customers.

## Certified connectors

Connectors that passed the compatibility suite are marked as certified. They are
supported by both engineering teams and receive a quarterly review.

## Beta connectors

Beta connectors are available to design partners only. Expect breaking changes
and a shorter support window.

## Requesting a new connector

Submit the partner intake form with the security questionnaire. The integrations
directory is reviewed every quarter by the partner engineering team.
