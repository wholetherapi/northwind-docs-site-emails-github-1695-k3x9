# Onboarding Guide

New teammates at Northwind Labs go through three short phases during their first month.
The goal is to get you productive without drowning you in process.

## Phase 1 — Accounts and access

Your buddy will file the access requests on your first morning. Most systems are
ready within one business day; the staging console usually takes two.

## Phase 2 — First contribution

Pick a `good-first-issue` from the team board. Ship it end to end: code, review,
release note. It is deliberately small so you can see the whole pipeline.

## Phase 3 — Own a surface area

By the end of week four you should own at least one recurring operational task.
Write down what you learned so the next person has an easier start.
